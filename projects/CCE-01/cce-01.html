CCE-01 — Cosmic Computational Epoch

CCE-01 explores the possibility of viewing cosmic history through the lens of computation.

The project models Differentiation → State → Structure → Complexity → Life → Mind → Machine → Now, presenting the evolution of distinguishable states as a continuously evolving computational timeline.

CCE-01 works alongside TRE-01 (Temporal Recursion Engine) to create a living visualization of modeled time—from an assumed computational origin, T₀, through an assumed first computation, T₁, to the present moment.

CCE-01 does not claim that the beginning of computation has been scientifically identified. The purpose is exploratory: to ask what happens when computation is treated as the ongoing evolution of distinguishable states and relationships.

CCE-01 is not presented as an established theory. It is a computational thought experiment rendered as a living system.

“If computation is fundamentally the evolution of distinguishable states, where should we place its beginning—and what does the resulting temporal scale look like?”
