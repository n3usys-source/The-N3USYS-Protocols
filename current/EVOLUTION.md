# N3USYS Evolution Map

N3USYS is a developing framework rather than a frozen v1 artifact. This map records architectural lineage without forcing later concepts backward into earlier work.

```text
Generation 0
    ↓
Early N3USYS Protocols
    ↓
Lattice / Resonance Era
    ↓
Protocol Formalization
    ↓
Empirical Integrity / Operator Corrective Action
    ↓
Intermediate Architectural Evolution
    ↓
Current N3USYS Control Architecture
    │
    ├── N3USYS Institute knowledge architecture
    ├── NCIE-01 — Computational Interface Experiment
    ├── CCE-01 — Computational Epoch research
    ├── TRE-01 — Time/visualization research
    └── UIF / other experimental systems
    │
    ▼
SSC-01 — Sentinel Systems Command
    │
    ▼
Future N3USYS Systems
```

## Architectural transition

The major transition is from N3USYS as primarily a **protocol concept** toward N3USYS as a **control architecture for systems that instantiate, test, demonstrate, or extend its principles**.

The current architecture therefore concerns more than prompt behavior. It establishes relationships among operator authority, intent, computational intelligence, controlled execution, verification, correction, memory/knowledge, and audit.

## Current research posture

The N3USYS Institute functions as a living research architecture in which theory can become model, model can become experiment, and experiment can produce evidence and revised doctrine. Notion serves as the institutional knowledge layer while GitHub provides the technical/public repository layer.

## Descendant systems

**NCIE-01** explores N3USYS principles through an actual computational interface in which the operator expresses intent and the system interprets and executes that intent through controlled mechanisms.

**SSC-01** extends the architecture into persistent command-and-control of a population of autonomous Sentinels, including monitoring, classification, coordination, response, verification, and audit.

These systems are descendants/applications of N3USYS rather than retroactive definitions of its early protocol generation.

## Versioning principle

A later generation supersedes an earlier generation through explicit documentation. It does not erase it.

> **Preserve the lineage. Version the doctrine. Test the implementation.**
