# Current N3USYS Terminology

This directory is the controlled vocabulary for the present-generation N3USYS framework.

## Core terms

**N3USYS** — A control architecture for Human–AI Joint Agency that governs the relationship among operator authority, intent, computational intelligence, constraints, verification, execution, correction, and audit.

**Operator** — The authorized human principal establishing or directing the mission/intent within the applicable authority boundary.

**Operator Primacy** — The principle that legitimate operator authority governs mission intent and consequential approval; computational capability does not independently create authority.

**Intent** — A structured representation of an objective, including relevant context, desired outcome, constraints, priorities, permissions, and success conditions.

**Control Plane** — The N3USYS layer responsible for maintaining authority, intent, constraints, coordination, alignment, integrity, and audit/feedback relationships around computational execution.

**Alignment** — The continuing state/process of maintaining consistency among active intent, authority, constraints, computational interpretation, and execution.

**Integrity** — The condition in which claims, state, capability, evidence, and uncertainty are represented without material fabrication or misleading conflation.

**Verification** — Evaluation of a material claim, state, assumption, or action against available evidence or a defined validation method.

**Correction** — An authoritative change to an erroneous or divergent interpretation, state, or output followed by re-evaluation and alignment recovery.

**Constraint** — A boundary limiting what may be considered, proposed, or executed within an active mission.

**Audit** — A reconstructable record of material decisions, evidence, state transitions, corrections, actions, and outcomes.

**Intelligence Layer** — The computational capability used by N3USYS, including models, agents, tools, services, or other processing systems.

## Historical terms

Terms such as **Intent Lattice**, **Resonance Engine**, **Resonance Alignment Cycle (RAC)**, **Operator Primacy Enforcement (OPE)**, and **Cognitive Execution Layer (CEL)** belong to earlier N3USYS generations unless a current specification explicitly redefines them.

When a term changes meaning, preserve the historical meaning in its original artifact and establish the new meaning here with an explicit version/date.

## Terminology rule

No historical term becomes current merely because it appears in an implementation, experiment, prompt, or discussion. Current terminology is established by explicit current specification.
