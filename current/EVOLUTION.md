# N3USYS Evolution Map

N3USYS is a developing framework rather than a frozen v1 artifact. This map establishes the repository's historical lineage without forcing later concepts backward into earlier work.

```text
Generation 0
    ↓
Early N3USYS Protocols
    ↓
Lattice / Resonance Era
    ↓
Protocol Formalization
    ↓
Empirical Integrity / Operator Corrective Action
    ↓
Intermediate Evolution
    ↓
Current N3USYS
    ↓
SSC-01 / Sentinel Architecture
    ↓
Future N3USYS Systems
```

## Interpretation

The exact boundaries between generations remain a research question. The important distinction is that the repository records **evolution**, not a single timeless definition.

Early lattice, resonance, operator-control, validation, and empirical-integrity artifacts should therefore be preserved as evidence of the framework's development.

## Versioning principle

A later generation supersedes an earlier generation through explicit documentation. It does not erase it.

## Current trajectory

The present N3USYS ecosystem is expanding beyond the original protocol concept toward a broader systems architecture. Related experimental systems such as SSC-01 and other N3USYS Institute projects should be treated as descendants or applications where their relationship is explicitly defined, rather than retroactively inserted into the early protocol documents.
